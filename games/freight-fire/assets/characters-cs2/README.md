# Original Counter-Strike 2 world characters

Two original character models (CT SAS and T Phoenix) and their 40 native animation clips are obtained from the public asset mirror referenced by the user-selected [ETO-ze/dust2-web](https://github.com/ETO-ze/dust2-web) project. Exact URLs, file sizes, and SHA-256 values are recorded in `sources.json` and checked before saving.

The source exporter selects third-person bodies and their default gloves, keeps the native metre scale and weapon anchor (`wpn`), and removes root-motion translations. The animation bundle contains directional movement, actual crouch poses, jump, rifle/pistol/knife poses and attacks, reloads, and the original `shared/death_chest_a.vnmclip_c` death animation. Freight Fire loads only tracks that exist in the selected character's skeleton. World weapons use their matching first-person idle gun-bone transforms and native wrist-to-gun matrices rather than the exported gun's bind pose. This removes the exported AWP root offset that placed the gun above the hands. Two-bone arm solving preserves the character's shoulder and elbow lengths while reaching those original grips and copying the original finger rotations. A relaxed hold lowers the grip in front of the abdomen and blends back to a shoulder hold while aiming, firing or reloading; the muzzle follows the player's yaw and pitch. The left hand remains free during the native reload clip. Knife and death retain their native body clips. Death plays once, holds the final pose, and adds game-world contact correction; the earlier custom particle ragdoll is no longer used.
